Added feedback prompting, new menu commands and settings
There is now a 1/7 chance you will get prompted to send feedback while on the main menu. A new feedback function has been added with colors and gradients. New setting called `feedback` in `Visuals` that enables/disables prompting, and `feedbackon`, `feedbackoff` as well as `forcefeedback` to go alongside it. Also added new check on boot.
